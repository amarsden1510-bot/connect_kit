■ National policy & funding
▪ First SBSG 2026 payments land today. DfE is distributing roughly £635m nationally this financial year through the Schools Budget Support Grant 2026, covering centrally employed local-authority staff plus early years and post-16 provision; the first tranche reaches local authorities today, with academies paid in November. It's a continuation of existing cost-pressure support rather than new Budget money - the mainstream-schools element has already been folded into the national funding formula for 2026-27. (GOV.UK - Department for Education)
→ For your school: Check your local authority's payment schedule if you're relying on this grant for centrally-employed-staff costs this term.
▪ Budget breakdown still pending. Two days on from Chancellor John Healey's Budget, DfE has yet to publish its own schools-funding breakdown of the settlement. Keep holding off on budget-planning assumptions, including on the scale of the SEND funding gap, until that lands. (Tes)
■ Sheffield & South Yorkshire
▪ Half term's last day. Today is the final day of Sheffield's autumn half term before pupils return on Monday 2 November. Academies can set their own calendar, so it's worth checking individual school dates rather than assuming a uniform city-wide return. (Sheffield City Council (via SaveMyExams term dates))
