■ Safeguarding & wellbeing
▪ Anti-Bullying Week 2026 opens today with 'Break the Silence' theme. The Anti-Bullying Alliance's annual week runs 16-20 November, its theme shaped by input from over 200 children and young people, opening with Odd Socks Day. Alliance director Martha Boateng says many children experiencing bullying still don't tell a trusted adult; NCB cites more than one in five children reporting being bullied each year (exact disclosure-rate figure not given in the source - flagged as approximate). (National Children's Bureau / Anti-Bullying Alliance)

■ National policy
▪ DfE's exclusion reform call for evidence enters its final week. The call for evidence on behaviour management - covering internal suspension, reintegration support partnerships and schoolwork provision during the first five days of a suspension or permanent exclusion - closes 19 November. It feeds the 'Every Child Achieving and Thriving' white paper's proposed time-limited internal suspension framework. (GOV.UK)
