■ National: a belonging gap for pupils with SEN support
▪ First wave of DfE's 'Growing Up in the 2020s' study published. The new nationally representative study of almost 8,000 Year 8 pupils - commissioned by DfE and run by Anna Freud, UCL and Ipsos - finds strong overall wellbeing (94% satisfied with life; 95-98% positive about friends and family), though a quarter show symptoms of mental ill-health. On school belonging, 63% overall agreed they felt they belonged, falling to 54% among pupils receiving SEN support (v 66% of peers) and 57% among girls (v 69% of boys). (GOV.UK / DfE, Anna Freud, UCL, Ipsos)
→ For your school: A useful evidence point for inclusion and pastoral teams making the case for targeted belonging work with SEN pupils and girls.
■ Sheffield: safety alert near Chelsea Park/Nether Edge
▪ High Storrs School wrote to parents after reports of a man approaching pupils. The Bents Green secondary told families on Wednesday that South Yorkshire Police had received reports of an adult male approaching and trying to engage young people in conversation near Chelsea Park on recent evenings. The school asked families to go through personal safety advice for journeys to and from school. (The Star)
