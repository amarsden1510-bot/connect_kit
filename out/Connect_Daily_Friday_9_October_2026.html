■ Ofsted & accountability: ASCL warns 'scope for negotiation is exhausted'
▪ ASCL's council met this week to decide on 'more assertive action' over Ofsted's impact on leader wellbeing. General secretary Pepe Di'Iasio said negotiation had been exhausted; Powell responded in writing that the current system is 'high-stakes and low-trust' and that she wants one that is 'more supportive'. (Tes)
→ For your school: Worth watching — a union escalation could pull any framework change forward.
■ Funding: a pay-funding row, and a high-needs deadline to note
▪ NASUWT says the 3.5% teacher pay rise isn't funded the way ministers claim. The union argues pension-scheme savings cited by Powell were already built into school budgets, so schools may have to find the money themselves; it wants the original funding table republished. (Personnel Today)
→ For your school: Check this term's budget against the funding assumptions NASUWT is disputing.
▪ Schools and colleges must request any 2027-28 high needs place changes by 6 November. This week's DfE Update confirms that if no request is made, 2026-27 place numbers simply roll over and set next year's funding allocation. (GOV.UK (DfE Update academies, 7 October 2026))
→ For your school: Confirm with your SENCo and finance lead whether specialist place numbers need updating.
■ Sheffield: a top Ofsted rating and a new NEET action plan
▪ High Storrs is now Sheffield's best-rated secondary under Ofsted's stricter framework. The Bents Green school, run by Minerva Learning Trust, was praised for pupils' confidence, extracurricular clubs, leadership and above-average attendance. (The Star)
▪ Sheffield has signed up to a near-90-council scheme to tackle NEET numbers. The move brings councils, schools, colleges, employers and health services together after figures showed one of England's higher NEET rates among 16-17 year-olds locally. (The Star)
→ For your school: Worth a word with your careers lead about how the scheme touches post-16 transition tracking.
