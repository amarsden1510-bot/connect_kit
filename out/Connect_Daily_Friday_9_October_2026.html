■ National: Ofsted extends pause on NPQ and early-career-teacher inspections
▪ Ofsted has confirmed a further pause on routine inspections of providers running National Professional Qualifications, now extended to also cover early career teacher programme providers. The pause - requested by the Minister for School Standards - runs through the rest of the 2026/27 academic year. Ofsted says it needs the time to bring NPQ and ECTP inspection into line with its current inspection approach, including a new toolkit, operating guide and report card, with sector engagement to follow. Routine inspections are expected to resume in 2027/28. (GOV.UK - Ofsted news)
→ For your school: Worth a line to CPD leads and anyone currently on an NPQ or supporting an ECT, so they know the lack of a provider inspection this year is expected, not a gap.
 
Jargon-buster - NPQ & ECTP
Both paused from routine Ofsted inspection this year:
NPQ (National Professional Qualification) - DfE-funded leadership and specialist qualifications for teachers and leaders. ECTP (Early Career Teacher Programme) - the structured two-year induction support for newly qualified teachers, formerly the ECF.
