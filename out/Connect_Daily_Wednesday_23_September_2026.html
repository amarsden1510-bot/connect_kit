■ Sheffield & South Yorkshire
▪ Sheffield approves extra £758k for new SEND facility at Stocksbridge Junior School. The finance and performance policy committee backed additional funding on Monday, taking the total to £823,000, for a Key Stage 2 Integrated Resource Provision for pupils with communication and interaction needs — citing rising local demand and families travelling long distances. Due to complete by May 2027; figures as reported ahead of published committee papers. (The Star)
→ For your school: A local capacity signal worth logging alongside Monday's exclusions discussion.
■ Ofsted & accountability
▪ NAHT calls for graded Ofsted inspections to be halted, citing risk to leader wellbeing. A survey of 5,000+ school leaders found nearly three-quarters feel inspection has worsened over the past year, one year on from the report-card reforms. General secretary Paul Whiteman said the framework poses 'a real and present danger' to leaders' wellbeing; the union wants grades replaced with a short narrative report pending a review. (ITV News)
→ For your school: Worth watching how this lands with leaders you support — inspection stress can ripple into inclusion decisions.
■ National funding
▪ Is the 'fully funded' teacher pay rise actually new money? Schools Week says no. DfE says the 3.5% pay award is fully funded via ~£500m/year in pension savings, but Schools Week argues schools already budgeted for those lower rates from April — so there's no genuine new money, and the saving varies school to school. (Schools Week)
→ For your school: Useful if finance leads ask why 'fully funded' doesn't match their budget reality.
