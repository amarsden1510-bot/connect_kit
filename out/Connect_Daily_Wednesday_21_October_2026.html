■ National policy & accountability
▪ Ofsted launches independent complaints pilot. Sir Martyn Oliver has announced that schools inspected in 2026/27 can ask an independent reviewer - contracted by DfE, separate from Ofsted - to look at whether an inspection's outcome was fair, not just how a complaint was handled. Where grading is in question, the reviewer can recommend a report card be set aside and the school reinspected. It follows NEU and other unions' votes of no confidence in Oliver's leadership this month. (Schools Week)
▪ Teacher pay dispute defused. The NEU has paused its strike ballot after Education Secretary Lucy Powell confirmed schools can keep savings from reduced pension employer contributions to cover the 3.5% 2026/27 pay award, rather than having them clawed back centrally - which the union is calling a fully-funded rise. (Schools Week)
■ Sheffield & South Yorkshire
▪ Firth Park/Parkwood outcome still awaited. As flagged yesterday, Sheffield's finance and performance committee met on Monday to decide on passing £602,000 of government funding to Lift Schools for three SEND rooms and 30 extra Year 7 places at Firth Park Academy, bundled with a temporary expansion at Parkwood Academy. No decision had been reported publicly as of this morning. (The Star)
→ For your school: Worth a call to the council or Lift Schools for the result if either is a feeder or partner.
