■ Behaviour, exclusions & attendance
▪ First hard data on autumn 2025/26 exclusions and suspensions. The DfE's autumn term 2025/26 release records around 3,300 permanent exclusions in England, down 12% on the roughly 3,700 recorded in autumn 2024/25, though the permanent exclusion rate held flat at 0.04% of pupils (4 in every 10,000). Suspensions fell 7% to around 312,000, with 47% lasting a single day or less. Flag: the rounded headline totals vary slightly from the underlying dataset (which shows 3,273 and 3,715 respectively), so treat any single figure as provisional pending the full characteristic breakdowns.
