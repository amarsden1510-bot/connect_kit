■ Sheffield & South Yorkshire
▪ Firth Park and Parkwood set for expansion. Sheffield's finance and performance committee meets today to approve passing £602,000 of government funding to Lift Schools, part of a £701,312 scheme giving Firth Park Academy three SEND rooms, refurbished creative-arts space and upgraded science labs - raising its admission number from 240 to 270. A temporary expansion at Parkwood Academy is bundled into the same paper, both aimed at a projected Year 7 places shortfall in 2027/28 and 2028/29. (The Star)
■ Pay & funding
▪ Pay Order laid, praying period now running. The STPCD 2026-27 Teachers' Pay Order was laid in Parliament on 16 October, confirming the 3.5% uplift (5% at the bottom of the unqualified range), backdated to 1 September. MPs now have a 21-day 'praying period' to raise objections, ending 6 November - in practice this has never been exercised, so arrears should follow once it lapses. (Local Government Association)
 
Jargon-buster - Praying period
Cropping up in today's pay story:
The 21 sitting days after a statutory instrument (like the Teachers' Pay Order) is laid in Parliament, during which an MP can table a motion against it. Rarely used, but it's why pay orders aren't final the day they're laid.
