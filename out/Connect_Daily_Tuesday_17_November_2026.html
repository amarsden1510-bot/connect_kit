■ Sheffield & South Yorkshire
▪ Three Sheffield secondary schools approved for expansion as council plans for a place shortage. Sheffield City Council's finance and performance policy committee has approved a package of works: a £1.1m temporary expansion adding 90 places at Hinde House in Shiregreen, a £4.5m three-storey extension at Forge Valley School in Stannington, and £200,000 to explore options at Yewlands Academy. Officers say Year 7 demand will keep rising across the city to 2029 and the council risks breaching its statutory duty to provide enough places if it doesn't act now. (The Star)
→ For your school: Worth checking whether any MAPs partner schools sit in these catchments - rising Year 7 numbers can squeeze SEND and inclusion provision fastest.
■ Behaviour & exclusions
▪ Exclusion reform call for evidence enters its final 48 hours. The DfE's call for evidence on internal suspension, reintegration support partnerships and schoolwork provision during the first days of a suspension or permanent exclusion closes Thursday 19 November. It feeds the 'Every Child Achieving and Thriving' white paper's proposed time-limited internal suspension framework. (GOV.UK)
→ For your school: Last chance for any Sheffield-side input before Thursday's deadline.
