■ Sheffield: city resets race equality commitments
▪ Education leaders and partners met to recommit to race equality work. Teachers, school leaders and partner organisations from across Sheffield came together last week to share learning and reaffirm joint commitments to race equality for children, young people and staff across the city's education system, building on the Anti Racist Education Alliance Sheffield (AREAS) and the city's Race Equality Commission work. (Sheffield City Council)
→ For your school: Worth checking whether MAPs schools were represented and what commitments came out of it.
■ National: DfE tries to define 'reasonable' on SEND
▪ Draft worked examples aim to clarify schools' Equality Act duties for SEND pupils. DfE has shared draft documents with trust leaders setting out what could count as a reasonable, or unreasonable, adjustment for pupils with SEND - for example a supervised early-arrival window, or access to a low-stimulus space for pupils with sensory processing needs. It's part of the push to make mainstream schools more inclusive ahead of wider SEND reform. (Tes / DfE)
→ For your school: Useful to flag to SENCOs shaping local adjustment policies ahead of formal guidance.
■ National: a cautionary tale on SEND, attendance and suspensions
▪ De Aston School, Lincolnshire, placed in special measures. Ofsted found attendance had been below the national average for three years with no sign of improvement, more than half of SEND pupils persistently absent (exact figure not given), and suspensions not being used in a way that improved behaviour, with some pupils suspended repeatedly in a short space of time. Safeguarding and the sixth form met the expected standard. (Grimsby Telegraph / Ofsted)
■ Sheffield: Fir Vale uniform dispute resolved
▪ Abaya row settled with new modesty-wear options. Fir Vale Academy has agreed three new clothing options - loose black trousers, a modesty skirt worn over trousers, or an ankle-length skirt - after weeks of pressure from parents and the local Muslim community over girls sent to internal exclusion for wearing abayas under its new uniform policy. (Hyphen)
 
Jargon-buster - reasonable adjustments
DfE's first worked examples (shared 1 Oct) try to pin down this fuzzy Equality Act duty.
Schools must take reasonable steps to remove disadvantage for disabled pupils. The draft guidance gives concrete examples for the first time - e.g. a supervised quiet arrival window, or a low-stimulus space for sensory needs.
