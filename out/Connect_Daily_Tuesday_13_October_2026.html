Jargon-buster - the Inclusion judgement
Since Ofsted's report-card framework began (from inspections on or after 10 November 2025), schools are graded across six core areas rather than one overall word.
Inclusion is the new standalone area - covering how well a school supports disadvantaged pupils, those with SEND, and those known to children's social care. It's graded on the same five-point scale as the others (Exceptional, Strong standard, Expected standard, Needs attention, Urgent improvement). Safeguarding sits apart from that scale, judged simply as met or not met.
