→ For your school: A useful citable source when escalating a case where interim provision has stalled or a specialist placement is delayed.

→ For your school: Check your managed-move and off-site-direction paperwork reflects the new requirements, not last year's version.
▪ Ofsted's September 2026 update to inspection handbooks is now live, covering early years, schools, independent schools, FE & skills and ITE providers.
→ For your school: Skim the schools-specific changes before your next inspection window, however distant it feels.

■ Inclusion & SEND
▪ "The Cost of Waiting" — new report on SEND delays. The report examines what happens when children with SEND can't access suitable education for prolonged periods. It argues schools and local authorities should weigh the wider educational, emotional and safeguarding implications of reduced timetables or waits for specialist placement, and calls for more consistent recognition of urgency, better data collection, and expedited holistic reviews where a child's vulnerability is increasing. (Nasen)
→ For your school: Worth having to hand for any EHCP-delay conversation with your local authority this term.
■ Behaviour, exclusions & attendance
▪ Exclusion guidance now the live framework for the new term. The updated statutory guidance on suspension and permanent exclusion — in force since 26 July — is the one schools are actually working to as pupils return. It reinforces that permanent exclusion should be a last resort, introduces a distinct 'safeguarding separation' category separate from disciplinary exclusion, and extends off-site-direction duties to academies and PRUs, alongside strengthened expectations for pupil voice in managed moves. (GOV.UK)
→ For your school: Confirm your behaviour policy and managed-move paperwork already reflect the safeguarding-separation distinction.
■ Ofsted & accountability
▪ September 2026 inspection-handbook updates published. Ofsted's annual round of updates to inspection materials under the renewed framework is now live, spanning early years, state-funded schools, non-association independent schools, FE & skills, and initial teacher education. (Ofsted)
■ Funding
▪ FSM expansion transitional protections start unwinding. Free school meal eligibility extended to all Universal Credit households from September; schools should expect eligibility-recheck activity through this term as transitional protections from the previous system phase out. (GOV.UK)
■ National policy
▪ DfE Update, 9 September 2026. The department's regular fortnightly bulletin to schools and trusts was published this week; these updates typically carry grant deadlines, data-collection windows and operational guidance rather than headline policy, so worth a scan for anything time-limited. (GOV.UK)
■ Sheffield & South Yorkshire
▪ Rotherham Schools Forum meets 18 September. The agenda includes the exclusion & suspensions annual report, an EHCP update, and early 2027/28 budget planning — a useful read-across given Sheffield's own high-needs budget pressures. (Rotherham MBC)
 
Jargon-buster - safeguarding separation
New terminology in this term's exclusion guidance:
Safeguarding separation — a temporary removal of a pupil from school premises for safeguarding reasons, which the DfE has now made explicit is NOT a disciplinary exclusion and must be managed through separate procedures, not the exclusions process.
