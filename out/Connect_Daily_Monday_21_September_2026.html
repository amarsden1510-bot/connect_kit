■ Sheffield & South Yorkshire
▪ Sheffield exclusions up 32% in a year — now second-worst among core cities. A report going to Tuesday's finance and performance policy committee found secondary exclusions averaged six a week in 2024-25, with over a third linked to a single serious incident rather than persistent disruption. A small number of schools account for a disproportionate share, and the Sheffield Inclusion Centre — statutory alternative provision from day six — is nearing capacity. Cllr Douglas Johnson called the rise 'really alarming'. Figures are as reported ahead of the committee meeting; worth checking against the published papers once available. (The Star)
→ For your school: Useful context if you're fielding questions about Inclusion Centre capacity or managed-move pressure this term.
■ National policy & funding
▪ £22.5m enrichment drive targets 400+ disadvantaged secondary schools. The Enrichment Expansion Programme funds music, sport, debating, engineering clubs and outdoor learning in mainstream, special and AP schools serving disadvantaged communities, building on the DfE's Enrichment Framework published in June. From this term, Ofsted assesses enrichment provision under personal development and wellbeing. (GOV.UK / DfE)
→ For your school: A lever worth flagging to schools still building out their enrichment offer.
