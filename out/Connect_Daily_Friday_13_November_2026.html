■ Sheffield & South Yorkshire
▪ Talbot strike still unresolved, over a week on. NEU members at Talbot Specialist School, the Norton Lees SEND school maintained directly by Sheffield City Council, walked out on 5 November over 'excessive workload, safe working practices and a lack of transparency in school governance', with further strike days called into this fortnight. No settlement has been reported; the council says it has asked the union 'to reconsider their proposed action'. (The Star / Yorkshire Post)
→ For your school: Worth checking directly with the school if you route SEND placements or respite support there this month.
▪ Council to rule on Hinde House expansion funding. Sheffield City Council's policy committee meets 16 November to approve £1.1m for a temporary 90-place expansion at Hinde House 2-16 School in Shiregreen - one of three secondary expansions, alongside Forge Valley and Yewlands, planned to meet a forecast shortage of Year 7 places across the city. (The Star)
 
Jargon-buster - in-school units & managed moves
Two terms likely to recur as DfE's exclusion reform call for evidence closes on 19 November:
An in-school unit is an on-site space pupils are moved to for part of the day instead of being suspended; a managed move is an agreed, time-limited transfer to a different mainstream school, used as an alternative to permanent exclusion.
