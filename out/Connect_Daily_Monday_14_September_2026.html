■ National policy & funding
▪ Unions warn schools are £4.5bn worse off since 2010. The Stop School Cuts coalition (ASCL, NEU, NAHT) published new real-terms analysis this week showing nearly three-quarters of schools have less per-pupil funding than in 2010, with over 3,700 schools down more than £1,000 per pupil and schools losing £300m in the last year alone. (Schools Week)
→ For your school: Sector-wide figures worth citing if you're pressing for inclusion funding to be protected in any in-year budget conversation.
■ Workforce & industrial relations
▪ NEU confirms formal strike ballot timetable. The union's national executive confirmed a formal ballot of the 280,000+ teachers it represents in England will run 3 October to 15 December over pay and funding, after an earlier indicative ballot saw over 90% of participating teachers say they'd be willing to take action; a parallel ballot of support staff was not agreed by the executive. (National Education Union)
→ For your school: A result in mid-December would land right before spring-term planning — worth flagging to SLT now.
