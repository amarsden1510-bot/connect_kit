■ Sheffield & South Yorkshire
▪ Firth Park and Parkwood expansion goes to committee. Sheffield's finance and performance committee met yesterday to approve passing £602,000 of government funding to Lift Schools, part of a £701,312 scheme giving Firth Park Academy three SEND rooms and 30 extra Year 7 places (240 to 270), bundled with a temporary expansion at Parkwood Academy. Both respond to a projected Year 7 places shortfall in 2027/28-2028/29. The committee's decision hadn't been reported publicly as of this morning. (The Star)
