■ Funding
▪ DfE confirms clawback of teacher pension savings. Schools Week reports the department has confirmed schools will not keep the full saving when the Teachers' Pension Scheme employer contribution rate falls from 28.6% to 17.6% from April 2027 - some of it will be clawed back centrally instead. Reported totals for the clawback have varied across different stages of coverage this autumn, so treat any single headline figure as provisional pending the DfE's own methodology note. (Schools Week)
→ For your school: Raise with finance/SLT now rather than waiting for the 2027 budget round - this changes medium-term planning assumptions for every school.
■ Behaviour, exclusions & attendance
▪ Official autumn exclusion figures due any day. The DfE's accredited statistics release covering suspensions and permanent exclusions in England for autumn term 2025/26 is scheduled for November 2026, with last year's equivalent published on 20 November - so the first official autumn data, rather than the unofficial early estimates that have been circulating, could land this week. (GOV.UK (Explore Education Statistics))
→ For your school: Worth checking the DfE release directly once published - the SEND breakdown within it feeds directly into MAPs placement and inclusion planning.
