■ Sheffield & South Yorkshire
▪ Strike dispute continues at Sheffield SEND school. NEU members at Talbot Specialist School, the Norton Lees school for pupils with SEND maintained directly by Sheffield City Council, walked out on 5 November citing 'excessive workload, safe working practices and a lack of transparency in school governance'. Five further strike days have been called over the following fortnight; the council says it has 'asked the NEU and its members... to reconsider their proposed action', with no resolution confirmed yet. (The Star / Yorkshire Post)
→ For your school: Worth flagging to any mainstream colleagues who route SEND placements or respite support through Talbot, in case further strike days this month disrupt provision.
