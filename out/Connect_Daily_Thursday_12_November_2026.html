Jargon-buster - inclusion strategy statement
A new statutory duty landing on every mainstream school in England.
By 31 December 2026, schools must publish a short (around 500-word) statement on their website setting out what they're doing - and spending, including any Inclusive Mainstream Fund money - to remove barriers to learning and participation. DfE issued a template in June; statements must be reviewed annually.
