■ Safeguarding & wellbeing
▪ Anti-Bullying Week sets 'Break the Silence' theme. The Anti-Bullying Alliance has confirmed Anti-Bullying Week 2026 runs 16-20 November, with this year's theme - shaped directly by over 200 children and young people - highlighting that many still don't tell anyone when they're bullied. Odd Socks Day on 16 November opens the week, and free assembly, lesson and discussion resources are already available. (NCB / Anti-Bullying Alliance)
→ For your school: Useful lead time to build into next week's assembly/PSHE planning if not already in the diary.
■ Behaviour & exclusions
▪ Exclusion reform feedback window narrows further. DfE's call for evidence on exclusion reform - covering behaviour strategies, in-school units and managed moves - now closes in 9 days, on 19 November. The accompanying autumn 2025/26 suspensions and permanent exclusions statistics are still due this month, with no exact publication date yet confirmed on GOV.UK. (GOV.UK)
→ For your school: Last call for the inclusion team to get any evidence in before the window closes.
 
Jargon-buster - Odd Socks Day
Seen it in Anti-Bullying Week materials and wondering what it means?
Odd Socks Day (16 Nov) is the Anti-Bullying Alliance's campaign opener - pupils and staff wear mismatched socks to mark that everyone is unique, used as an icebreaker for assembly conversations about bullying and difference.
