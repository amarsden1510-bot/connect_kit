■ Sheffield & South Yorkshire
▪ Council points to MAT engagement as the next step on rising exclusions. Following Monday's committee report on the 32% rise in Sheffield exclusions, the council's strategic director for children's services said she had met multi-academy trust chief executives, who 'showed a real commitment' to keeping children in school. Cllr Douglas Johnson flagged that the council's exclusions team too often becomes involved only at the point of exclusion, rather than earlier when a placement is at risk. (The Star)
→ For your school: Useful if you're approached about earlier support before a managed move or exclusion is finalised.
