■ National policy & funding
▪ All eyes on tomorrow's Budget. Chancellor John Healey delivers his first Budget on Wednesday, with the Office for Budget Responsibility publishing its updated Economic and Fiscal Outlook alongside it. Healey has said living-cost support will be "targeted and temporary" rather than universal; nothing has been confirmed either way yet on further top-ups to mainstream or high-needs school funding, so treat any pre-Budget schools figures circulating this week as speculation until it's delivered. ■ Sheffield & South Yorkshire
▪ Sheffield in the final four for City of Culture 2029. Sheffield is competing with Blackpool, Milton Keynes and Wrexham for the UK City of Culture 2029 title after making the shortlist; all four get at least £125,000 to develop their bids, with £10 million for the eventual winner. Council leader Fran Belbin has pointed to potential benefits for young people's career pathways and aspirations as part of the city's bid. (The Star)
 
Jargon-buster - OBR
Wednesday's Budget comes with an independent verdict attached.
The Office for Budget Responsibility (OBR) is the UK's independent fiscal watchdog. It publishes an Economic and Fiscal Outlook alongside every Budget, checking the Chancellor's sums against the government's own borrowing and debt rules - its verdict often shapes how much room a Chancellor really has to spend.
