Jargon-buster - reduced timetable
Coming up in Rotherham's still-unpublished Schools Forum papers, and a term worth having ready.
A 'reduced' or 'part-time' timetable is a temporary, formally agreed cut to a pupil's school hours — usually while additional SEND or behaviour support is arranged. It should never be used as an informal way to manage a difficult pupil out of full-time education, and DfE guidance expects it to be time-limited, reviewed regularly, and recorded with parental agreement.
