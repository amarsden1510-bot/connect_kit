■ NEETs & transitions
▪ NEET total dips on the quarter, but the annual trend is going the wrong way for young women ONS estimates 959,000 16-24-year-olds in the UK were NEET in July-September 2026 - down 22,000 on the previous quarter, but up 17,000 on a year earlier. The breakdown: roughly 476,000 young women NEET (up ~41,000 on the year) against about 483,000 young men (down ~24,000). Youth Futures Foundation says the figures underline the need for sustained, evidence-based action rather than one-off interventions - relevant context for schools tracking post-16 destinations and early identification of pupils at risk of disengaging. (ONS)
