■ Funding
▪ Unions: schools £4.5bn worse off since 2010. The Stop School Cuts coalition's report, launched this week, puts real-terms losses at £4.5bn over 16 years, with over 3,700 schools down more than £1,000 per pupil and 92% of secondaries worse off than in 2010. (Schools Week)
→ For your school: Treat the topline figures as a campaign framing rather than official statistics.
■ Inclusion & SEND
▪ EOTAS consultation deadline approaching. The government's proposals on education otherwise than at school for pupils with EHC plans close for responses on 18 September, feeding into a planned Education for All Bill; existing EHCPs stay protected until 2030. (GOV.UK)
■ Sheffield & South Yorkshire
▪ Rotherham forum to review exclusions and EHCPs. Rotherham's Schools Forum meets 18 September to consider its exclusion & suspensions annual report, an EHCP update and 2027/28 budget planning — a useful read-across given Sheffield's own high-needs budget pressures. (Rotherham MBC)
 
Jargon-buster - EOTAS
Cropping up in this month's SEND consultation:
Education Otherwise Than at School — when a council arranges a child's EHC-plan provision somewhere other than a school or college, e.g. at home or via a mixed package of providers.
