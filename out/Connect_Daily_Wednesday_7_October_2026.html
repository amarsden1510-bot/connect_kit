■ National: near-daily sexual offences recorded in or near schools, Guardian analysis finds
▪ FOI data from two-thirds of forces shows thousands of recorded offences against under-13s. At least 8,500 sexual offences against under-13s were recorded on or near school premises in England and Wales between 2021 and 2025 - nearly five a day - including close to 2,000 rapes or attempted rapes; about two-thirds of under-13 victims were girls, and most identified alleged offenders were themselves children. Only 33 of 43 forces supplied data, so the true total is almost certainly higher. (The Guardian)
→ For your school: Worth checking harmful sexual behaviour training and reporting routes are current, especially for younger pupils and SEND pupils who may be less able to flag concerns.
■ Sheffield: GCSE table lands ahead of Y7 admissions deadline
▪ A round-up of Sheffield secondaries' GCSE grades arrives as the local application deadline nears. The Star's look at Sheffield secondary schools' GCSE performance lands with the city's non-selective deadline to apply for September 2027 Year 7 places approaching on 16 October (national deadline 31 October) - useful context for parents weighing options this half term. (The Star)
→ For your school: Pastoral and admissions leads may want it on hand for parent queries before half term.
