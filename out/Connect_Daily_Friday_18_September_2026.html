■ Inclusion, SEND & alternative provision
▪ SEND/EOTAS consultations close today. The DfE's linked consultations on reforming education otherwise than at school and on a new local SEND inclusion formula — which would front-load more SEND funding to mainstream schools rather than via top-ups — both close at 11.59pm tonight. Existing EHCPs stay protected until 2030; the government's response is expected in autumn. (GOV.UK)
→ For your school: If any MAPs partner school hasn't responded, today is the last chance.
■ Sheffield & South Yorkshire
▪ New learning places strategy aims to tackle SEND demand and exclusions together. Councillors are considering a city-wide learning places strategy proposing extra specialist SEND places and a joined-up approach linking exclusions, alternative provision and SEND placement decisions — responding to a sustained rise in both exclusions and SEND demand. Children with SEND, from disadvantaged backgrounds and from ethnic minority communities remain over-represented in exclusion figures, the report notes. (Sheffield City Council)
→ For your school: Flag to partner schools once a committee date is confirmed — this is the strategy that will shape AP commissioning locally.
■ National policy & funding
▪ DfE pushes back two autumn deadlines. This week's DfE Update moves the operational deadline for autumn-census free school meal eligibility checks from 1 October to 2 November, giving schools more time to implement the new Universal Credit-based FSM expansion, while academy trusts get until 24 September for budget forecast returns. (GOV.UK (DfE Update, 16 September 2026))
→ For your school: Useful breathing room if FSM re-checks are still in progress for MAPs schools.
 
Jargon-buster - EOTAS
Cropping up a lot in today's SEND coverage:
Education Otherwise Than At School — provision for children who can't attend a mainstream or special school placement, e.g. home tuition, hospital education or approved online providers. Tonight's consultation proposes new statutory guidance for how councils commission and quality-assure it.
