■ National: DfE's October update revises FSM/NRPF guidance, publishes new high-needs data
▪ Yesterday's DfE Update for academies revises how schools check free school meal eligibility for families with no recourse to public funds, and publishes fresh high-needs figures. The 7 October update sets out revised guidance on free school meals for children in families with no recourse to public funds, publishes spring 2026 census figures for high-needs pupils in special schools and free schools, and opens the 2027/28 high-needs place-change process for local authorities. It also confirms the autumn census FSM eligibility recheck deadline has moved from 1 October to 2 November. (GOV.UK - DfE Update (academies), 7 October 2026)
→ For your school: Worth flagging to your FSM/pastoral team given the moved deadline, and feeding into any high-needs place-planning conversations with the LA.
■ Funding: pupil premium quarter 2 lands with academies today
▪ Academies open at 1 April 2026 are paid today's quarter 2 instalment of 2026/27 pupil premium funding. Trusts can now download their quarter 2 allocation statements via DfE Sign-in; the next instalment, for academies open at 1 September 2026, follows on 11 January 2027. Maintained schools were paid on 30 September. (GOV.UK - Pupil premium: conditions of grant 2026 to 2027)
■ South Yorkshire: Doncaster Catholic primaries seek academy conversion
▪ DfE's Yorkshire & Humber office lists two Doncaster Catholic primaries among this month's academy conversion decisions. St Joseph and St Teresa's Catholic Primary School, and Our Lady of Perpetual Help Catholic Primary School, have applied to convert to academy status and join St Francis Catholic Multi Academy Trust, with a decision due this month. (GOV.UK - Regions Group school decisions: Yorkshire and the Humber)
 
Jargon-buster - NRPF
Cropping up in this week's DfE update:
No Recourse to Public Funds - families, often with insecure immigration status, who can't claim most mainstream benefits. They have a separate route into free school meals with its own income thresholds, which DfE revised this week.
