■ Sheffield & South Yorkshire
▪ Firth Park and Parkwood expansion decision still unconfirmed. Sheffield's finance and performance committee met Monday to decide on passing £602,000 of government funding to Lift Schools - part of a £701,312 scheme giving Firth Park Academy three SEND rooms and 30 extra Year 7 places (240 to 270) - alongside a smaller, temporary expansion at Parkwood Academy. Both respond to a projected Year 7 places shortfall in 2027/28-2028/29. Three days on, no outcome has been reported publicly. (The Star)

Jargon-buster - ICR
Ofsted announced a new complaints route for schools last week, worth knowing before it reaches your corridor.
The Independent Complaint Review (ICR) is a pilot covering schools inspected in 2026/27. Unlike the current ICASO process, which only checks whether Ofsted followed its own procedure, the ICR can also assess whether the complaint outcome itself - including a published grade - was fair, and can trigger a fresh inspection.
