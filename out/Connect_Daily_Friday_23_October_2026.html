Jargon-buster - Permanent Secretary
Today's top story is about the DfE's new Permanent Secretary - a role that shapes education policy long after ministers move on.
The Permanent Secretary is the most senior civil servant in a government department, running it day-to-day and advising ministers. Unlike the Education Secretary (currently Lucy Powell), a political appointee, the Permanent Secretary stays in post across changes of government and minister.
