■ Labour conference: two lines for schools
▪ New vocational GCSEs confirmed for 2029. Education secretary Lucy Powell used her conference speech to confirm vocational GCSEs — covering subjects like construction, child development and business — sitting alongside traditional GCSEs from as early as September 2029. She rejected the 'dumbing down' charge, calling them 'very hard subjects' with real maths and problem-solving content. (LBC)
→ For your school: Four years out, but worth a mention to your curriculum/options lead as a future pathway for pupils the current GCSE offer doesn't serve well.
▪ Schools told to 'go further' on misogyny. Powell said schools must move beyond this year's RSHE strategy and embed healthy relationships 'into every part of school life, not just RSHE lessons', including teaching on deepfakes, image-based abuse and online harassment. Her line: 'We simply cannot just teach our way out of misogyny.' (Tes)
→ For your school: Worth checking with your PSHE/RSHE lead that deepfakes and image-based abuse are explicitly covered, not folded into a generic online-safety unit.
