■ National policy & funding
▪ Budget lands light for schools. Chancellor John Healey's first Budget, delivered yesterday alongside the OBR's updated forecast, included few new schools-specific announcements. The OBR's figures point to councils' cumulative SEND deficits continuing to widen sharply over the next couple of years - treat the exact scale as a reported, not independently verified, figure until DfE's own breakdown lands. Commentary also flagged that mainstream per-pupil funding is on track to fall in real terms, as funding is held flat against falling pupil numbers, with no new grant confirmed to cover schools' rising minimum-wage costs. (Tes)
→ For your school: Hold off on budget-planning assumptions until the DfE publishes its own schools-funding breakdown of yesterday's settlement.
▪ SEND still the pressure point. Education Secretary Lucy Powell inherits a department where SEND cost pressures remain the dominant budget issue for schools and councils alike; expect more detail on how the Budget settlement feeds into the wider SEND reform programme in the coming weeks rather than today. (Schools Week)
■ Sheffield & South Yorkshire
▪ Still half term. Sheffield schools remain closed for the autumn half term until Monday 2 November. Academies can set their own dates, so it's worth double-checking individual school calendars before assuming a city-wide return. (Sheffield City Council (via SaveMyExams term dates))
