■ National policy & funding
▪ Biggest school food shake-up in a decade. New standards will limit food and drink high in fat, salt and sugar and put more fresh fruit, vegetables and wholegrains on every plate. Schools have a year to prepare before the rules take effect in September 2027, with some secondary-specific measures phased in by 2028. (GOV.UK)
→ For your school: Long lead-in, but a good moment to start the catering-contract conversation.
■ Sheffield & South Yorkshire
▪ Nine Sheffield schools and nurseries get fresh Ofsted ratings. The Star's latest roundup covers inspections from the summer through to the first week of September: Swallownest Primary and Bankwood Community were both praised, while Stocksbridge High School was criticised over 'unacceptable' attendance and disadvantaged pupils leaving without basic qualifications. (The Star)
→ For your school: Worth a read if any partner schools are due inspection soon — a live sense of what's landing hardest under the current framework.
■ Safeguarding & wellbeing
▪ Thirlwall Inquiry's final report published. The inquiry into failures at the Countess of Chester Hospital reported this week; the Health Secretary called it "thorough and devastating" and pledged action on whistleblowing and information-sharing failures. It's an NHS story, not a schools one, but it lands the same week the DfE's own information-sharing duty comes into focus ahead of its 30 September commencement. (GOV.UK)
→ For your school: Not schools-specific, but useful background if your safeguarding lead is reviewing how concerns get escalated and shared across agencies.
 
Jargon-buster - EOTAS
Cropping up a lot as the DfE's SEND consultations close this Friday.
"Education Otherwise Than At School" — the legal route for children who can't attend a mainstream or special school placement. The current consultation asks whether it should also cover some children no longer eligible for an EHCP.
