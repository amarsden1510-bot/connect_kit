■ Sheffield & South Yorkshire
▪ NEU strike action hits Talbot Specialist School. Staff at Talbot Specialist School - one of the few Sheffield schools still maintained directly by the council rather than a trust - walked out on Thursday 5 November, with the NEU threatening several more strike days across November. The union cites excessive workload and unsafe working practices; the council has asked members to reconsider. (The Star (Sheffield))
→ For your school: Flag to the inclusion team if ongoing disruption affects SEND placement or referral capacity at Talbot over the coming weeks.
■ Behaviour & exclusions
▪ Exclusion reform feedback window narrows. DfE's call for evidence on exclusion reform - covering behaviour strategies, in-school units and managed moves - closes in 10 days, on 19 November. The accompanying autumn 2025/26 suspensions and permanent exclusions statistics are still due this month, with no exact publication date confirmed yet. (Local Government Lawyer / GOV.UK)
→ For your school: Last call for the inclusion team to get any evidence in before the window closes.
