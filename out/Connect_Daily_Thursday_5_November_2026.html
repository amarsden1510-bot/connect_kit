Jargon-buster - internal suspension
Comes up throughout DfE's live exclusion-reform call for evidence.
Removing a pupil from their usual class or timetable for a fixed period while keeping them on the school site, supervised separately, rather than issuing a formal suspension. DfE is asking the sector whether wider, more consistent use of it could reduce reliance on off-site suspensions.
