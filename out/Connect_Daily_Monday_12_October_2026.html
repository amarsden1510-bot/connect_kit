■ Ofsted & accountability: interim evaluation finds inspections landing better, but workload still a worry
▪ Friday's independently-commissioned interim evaluation of Ofsted's renewed inspection framework found most inspected providers rate the experience positively so far. IFF Research's interim findings, covering the first four survey waves since the new framework and report cards launched, found 88% of inspected providers agreed their inspection was conducted respectfully, 76% collaboratively and 78% empathetically, and 81% said their report card accurately described what it's like to be a pupil or learner there. Workload and wellbeing remain flagged as ongoing pressures, though most respondents said inspectors took steps to reduce stress on the day. (GOV.UK - Independent research finds Ofsted's new-look inspections are more collaborative and respectful, 9 October 2026)
→ For your school: Useful to share with staff anxious about inspection day - the evidence suggests the new approach is landing better than the old single-word judgements, even if workload concerns haven't gone away.
