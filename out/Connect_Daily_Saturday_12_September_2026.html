■ Inclusion & SEND
▪ "The Cost of Waiting" — new report on SEND delays. The report examines what happens when children with SEND can't access suitable education for prolonged periods, arguing schools should weigh the wider educational, emotional and safeguarding implications of reduced timetables or waits for specialist placement, and calls for more consistent recognition of urgency across local authorities. (Nasen)
