■ National policy & funding
▪ Government confirms new School Food Standards. Following a consultation that closed in June, DfE has finalised reforms banning deep-fried food outright and capping high-sugar ‘grab and go’ options, with salad or vegetables required in every main meal. A new national enforcement mechanism will monitor compliance, though the standards themselves don't take effect until September 2027. (GOV.UK)
→ For your school: Long lead-in time, but catering contracts and menu planning may be worth an early look.
■ Sheffield & South Yorkshire
▪ Latest Ofsted round-up for Sheffield schools and nurseries. Nine local settings had reports published this week. Stocksbridge High School was criticised for ‘unacceptable’ attendance records and disadvantaged pupils often leaving without basic qualifications; Bankwood Community was praised for recovering fully from a 2022 ‘inadequate’ rating; Swallownest Primary also impressed inspectors. (The Star)
