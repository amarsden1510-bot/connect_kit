■ Behaviour, exclusions & inclusion
▪ Exclusion reform evidence call closes 19 November. DfE's call for evidence on school exclusion reform - covering behaviour management strategies, in-school units and managed moves - closes in under three weeks. Responses will feed into the government's wider 'Every child achieving and thriving' reform programme, so it's a live channel for sector views on AP and inclusion practice rather than a done deal. (GOV.UK)
→ For your school: Flag the 19 November deadline to anyone in the MAPs network who wants to submit evidence.
■ Pay & funding - correction
▪ NEU ballot is paused, not live. Contrary to a line that's been doing the rounds, the NEU's national executive paused its planned formal strike ballot over pay, funding and workload in late September, after the government ruled out clawing back schools' pension-contribution savings. No new ballot date has been set, so the 15 December closing date some coverage still cites is out of date. (LBC / NEU)
■ Sheffield & South Yorkshire
▪ Back from half term, on a high. Sheffield's community and voluntary-controlled schools return today. Over half term, The Star's round-up of recent Ofsted outcomes highlighted a strong run for the city, with ten schools and nurseries inspected describing them as happy, welcoming and ambitious. (The Star)
